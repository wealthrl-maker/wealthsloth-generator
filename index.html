Step 2 — MR.TIE Pose

Pose must match the trigger. Never repeat the same pose in consecutive thumbnails.

Default / Hero
Hands clasped, facing camera. Calm. Authoritative.
Side Profile
Facing left. Arms crossed or clasped. Contemplative.
Thinking
Hand raised to chin. Head tilted slightly. Eyes forward.
Pointing
One arm extended, finger pointing at screen or chart.
Seated
Upright in chair. Hands on desk or lap. Never slouched.
Back View
Full suit back visible. Looking at cityscape or screen ahead.
Step 3 — Thumbnail Text

Max 5 words. Must deepen the gap — not answer it. All caps, Bebas Neue or Montserrat Bold.

⚠ Over 5 words — thumbnails with more than 5 words lose CTR at small sizes
Step 4 — Scene Context

Describe any props, objects, or environment. MR.TIE character prompt is auto-included.
